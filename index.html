Guan-Horng Liu

Research Scientist @ Meta Superintelligence Labs (MSL)

I develop methods for learning generative models with scientific structures. Data-driven methods such as Schrödinger bridges [I2SB, DeepGSB] have advanced diffusion models with optimality structures and physics priors. Data-efficient methods like adjoint-based diffusion samples [ASBS, DAM] have enabled scientific exploration driven by reward and energy functions. Lately, I’ve been exploring RL science in post-training LLMs.

My research has led to large-scale applications in

  • Generative AI: mathematical reasoning, image generation & restoration & unsupervised translation, watermarked generation.
  • AI for Science: conformer generation, transition states & paths sampling, protein folding, scRNA sequencing, particle physics.
  • AI for Society: population modeling, opinion depolarization, mean-field games.

My research has received multiple recognitions, including 5+ Oral/Spotlight presentations [accept rate ≤ 3%] in major ML conferences (ICLR, ICML, NeurIPS), cover feature in Nature Machine Intelligence, coverage in MIT News & the MetaAI blog, and GaTech AE Graduate Research Fellowship. I’m deeply grateful to my collaborators, mentees, and interns from FAIR, NVIDIA Research, Georgia Tech, MIT, Cornell, and KAIST.
